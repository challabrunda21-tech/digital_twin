## 2. Distinction of Verification Categories

To enable another AI assistant or technical auditor to independently verify this documentation package, every statement in this analysis belongs to one of three categories:

### 1. Verified by Execution (Highest Reliability)
- Execution of `python -m pytest tests/ -v`: Confirmed that all 32 automated unit and integration tests pass cleanly with zero errors in 1.05 seconds.
- Route checks: Confirmed that `app.py` successfully mounts all 16 endpoints and serves `static/index.html`.
- Calculation checks: Confirmed that `PhysicsTwinEngine`, `DiagnosticAIEngine`, `PrognosticEngine`, and `PrescriptiveEngine` produce mathematically consistent outputs matching formulas in `03_METRICS_AND_ALGORITHMS.md`.

### 2. Verified by Source Code Inspection (Direct Evidence)
- The exact equations for current, heat, pressure, autoencoder reconstruction error, self-attention, and RUL degradation are directly quoted with exact line numbers from `core/*.py`.
- The SVG elements, canvas context drawing methods, and DOM event listeners are verified line-by-line from `static/js/*.js` and `static/index.html`.

### 3. Documented Limitations & Assumptions (Honest Engineering Disclosures)
- The physical machine is simulated via pre-recorded CSV testbed streams rather than an active physical motor wired to the laptop.
- High-frequency 500-sample vibration waveforms are reconstructed in real time from the 1 Hz RMS value to drive FFT power spectra.
- Variable Frequency Drive derating modulates an in-memory setpoint and logs Modbus-style holding register actions rather than transmitting hardware 4-20mA signals to an industrial inverter.
